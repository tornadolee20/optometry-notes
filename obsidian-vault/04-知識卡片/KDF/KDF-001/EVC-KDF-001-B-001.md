# EVC-KDF-001-B-001｜近視控制框架鏡片的中央、離軸與適應證據

## Research Question

[[KDF-001-B-001]]：近視控制框架鏡片會如何影響中央／離軸視覺品質與適應？現有研究是否足以回答孩子的真實生活功能？

## Search Strategy

2026-08-13 以 PubMed／PMC 進行目標式搜尋。優先納入兒童隨機試驗與兒童比較研究；成人單次 crossover 只用於解釋離軸機制，不直接外推兒童生活安全。來源開始重複出現「中央多半保留、特定離軸／低對比條件可能下降」後停止搜尋。

## What We Know

1. Straight-ahead central performance is often preserved or acceptable in the studied designs, especially after longer adaptation periods.
2. Some designs produce measurable reductions under low-contrast, low-luminance, or off-axis viewing conditions.
3. A small child study found mildly reduced mid-peripheral near acuity with DIMS but no detectable change in static visual-field sensitivity within the tested 30-degree field.
4. Results depend on lens design, gaze path, luminance, contrast, outcome, testing time, and sample.

## What We Do Not Know

- Whether laboratory differences translate into meaningful problems with stairs, uneven ground, sport, night mobility, rapid gaze shifts, or classroom transitions.
- Which children adapt slowly or do not adapt, and which optical, frame-fitting, behavioral, or individual variables predict that trajectory.
- Whether differences between products can be compared fairly using a common dynamic functional test battery.
- How often early subjective discomfort changes adherence outside clinical trials.

## Conflicting or Condition-Dependent Evidence

The evidence is not a simple yes/no conflict. Central and conventional visual tests are commonly preserved, while peripheral-zone or difficult-condition tests sometimes show decrements. These can both be true because they test different gaze paths, contrasts, luminance, designs, and times after dispensing.

Several studies include manufacturer collaboration, employment, or research funding. That does not invalidate the data, but it increases the importance of independent replication and design-specific wording.

## What Can Be Concluded

- `C2`: Current studies support that some near-peripheral or off-axis visual metrics can decline under specific lens designs and test conditions, while central visual performance is often preserved.
- `C2`: Some short-term differences diminish over months in specific child trials, consistent with adaptation or test familiarity, but the mechanism and individual trajectories are not settled.

## What Cannot Be Concluded

- These lenses generally impair children's daily safety or normal activity.
- Preserved central acuity proves that every child has no functional visual cost.
- Findings from one lens family, adult laboratory study, or industry-sponsored program apply to every myopia-control spectacle design.
- The field observation framework in [[FOC-KDF-001-B-001]] is a validated questionnaire or efficacy measure.

## Evidence Strength

**C2**. The source set includes child randomized evidence and direct visual-function testing, but it is heterogeneous, design-specific, often laboratory-based, and incomplete for dynamic real-world outcomes. Gate 1 Evidence Review remains pending.
