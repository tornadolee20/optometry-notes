# CASE-CI-CHILD-001

## 案例目的

測試系統是否能在兒童 symptomatic CI-like pattern 中，輸出保守且 evidence-linked 的 clinician-reviewable candidate management option。

## 模擬族群

9–17 歲兒童。

## 需要輸入的資料

- CISS elevated
- NPC receded
- near PFV reduced
- near exophoria supportive
- refraction reviewed
- accommodation reviewed
- no red flags

## 預期 pattern

May be consistent with symptomatic convergence insufficiency pattern.

## 預期 management option

可以連到：

[[CI_Office_Based_Therapy]]

但必須保留 clinician review wording。

## 不允許輸出

- 系統診斷 CI
- 保證視覺訓練有效
- 自動建議開始治療
- 套用到所有年齡
- 說 home-only exercise 與 office-based therapy 等效

## 預期安全文字

候選處置必須提到：

- 9–17 歲研究族群
- office-based vergence/accommodative therapy with home reinforcement
- randomized trial evidence
- applicability requires clinician review
- evidence limitation
