# Report Language Guard

同一個 clinical concept，必須分成不同受眾語言。

## 四層語言

1. Clinician wording
2. Patient wording
3. Management wording
4. Marketing wording

## 核心原則

臨床端可以精準。
患者端要白話。
處置端要有證據與限制。
行銷端不能過度宣稱。

## 可以說

- suggests
- may be consistent with
- clinician-reviewable
- candidate management option
- evidence applies to this studied population
- requires clinical review

## 不能說

- definitely diagnoses
- guarantees
- cures
- automatically treats
- proven for everyone
- replaces clinician judgement

## CI 範例

### Clinician wording

Findings may be consistent with a symptomatic convergence insufficiency pattern and should be reviewed alongside symptoms, refractive status, accommodation, near point of convergence, and positive fusional vergence.

### Patient wording

你的雙眼在近距離工作時，可能需要比一般人更用力，所以閱讀或看螢幕時，可能比較容易累或不穩。

### Management wording

For clinician review: in children aged 9 to 17 with study-like symptomatic convergence insufficiency, office-based vergence/accommodative therapy with home reinforcement has randomized trial evidence. Applicability depends on the full clinical context.

### Marketing wording

Helps optometrists explain binocular vision findings in a clearer, more structured way.
