# DQ-KDF-001-002 Evidence Search Plan

## Target KDF Card

- Discovery Question: `[[DQ-KDF-001-002]]`
- Question: 標準中央視力正常，是否足以代表配戴近視控制鏡片兒童在真實生活中的視覺舒適度、任務表現與配戴依從性？
- Status: candidate / human_approved: false
- Origin cards:
  - `[[EVC-KDF-001-B-001]]`
  - `[[MKC-KDF-001-B-001]]`

## Core Clinical Framing

中央視力正常只能代表標準條件下的清晰度；對配戴近視控制鏡片的兒童而言，仍需追蹤低對比、低照度、離軸觀看、動態任務、長時間配戴後的主觀舒適度，以及是否影響配戴意願與依從性。

本計畫不是要證明近視控制鏡片會造成日常功能問題，而是要釐清：標準中央視力是否足以代表真實生活視覺表現，以及目前證據缺口在哪裡。

## Primary Research Question

標準中央視力正常的配戴近視控制鏡片兒童，是否仍可能在真實生活相關條件下出現視覺舒適度、任務表現或配戴依從性的落差？

## Subquestions

1. 中央高對比視力正常時，低對比或低照度視覺表現是否仍可能下降？
2. 離軸、周邊區域或側向注視下的視覺表現，是否與中央視力不同？
3. 閱讀、視覺搜尋、黑板／課本切換、樓梯、運動等任務是否有被直接研究？
4. 配戴時間、適應期與疲勞是否影響主觀舒適度或任務表現？
5. 視覺品質、舒適度與生活品質是否影響實際配戴時長與依從性？
6. 鏡片設計、配適條件或個體視覺功能是否能解釋哪些孩子較容易出現不適應？

## PICO / PECO

### Population

- 主要：配戴近視控制框架鏡片的兒童與青少年。
- 次要：年輕成人短期 crossover 機制研究，只能用於補充離軸或光學機制，不可直接外推兒童長期生活適應。

### Exposure / Intervention

- DIMS / defocus incorporated multiple segments
- HAL / highly aspherical lenslets
- SAL / slightly aspherical lenslets
- DOT / diffusion optics technology
- IORC / individualized ocular refraction customization spectacle lenses
- 其他 lenslet、defocus ring、peripheral defocus 或 contrast-modulating spectacle designs

### Comparator

- Single vision spectacle lenses
- Baseline habitual correction
- Other myopia control spectacle designs
- Orthokeratology or contact lens comparators only as secondary context

### Outcomes

#### Primary Outcomes

- central high-contrast visual acuity
- low-contrast visual acuity
- contrast sensitivity function
- photopic / scotopic / low-luminance visual acuity
- mid-peripheral or off-axis visual acuity
- peripheral-zone visual performance
- subjective quality of vision / comfort
- adherence / compliance / wear time

#### Secondary Outcomes

- reading performance
- visual search task performance
- accommodative facility / lag / microfluctuation
- stereoacuity / phoria / binocular function
- visual symptoms / ghosting / blur / distortion
- quality of life
- sport, stairs, mobility, classroom transitions, dynamic daily tasks

## Inclusion Criteria

- Human studies.
- Pediatric myopia control spectacle lens studies prioritized.
- Studies reporting visual function, visual performance, quality of vision, symptoms, quality of life, comfort, compliance, adherence, or wear time.
- Randomized trials, comparative studies, crossover studies, observational studies, and relevant reviews.
- Adult crossover studies only when they directly test off-axis, peripheral-zone, reading, visual search, or optical-mechanism outcomes.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Pure myopia progression efficacy studies without any visual function, comfort, safety, adherence, or quality-of-life outcome.
- Contact lens or atropine-only papers unless used as background for compliance / quality-of-life framing.
- Optical bench or animal studies unless they explain a mechanism directly relevant to human visual function.
- Studies that cannot distinguish spectacle lens design or outcome condition.

## Evidence Buckets

### Bucket 1: Central VA Preserved

Studies showing central high-contrast VA or standard visual function remains acceptable under studied designs.

Purpose: avoid overclaiming that myopia-control spectacle lenses broadly impair vision.

### Bucket 2: Condition-Specific Differences

Studies showing differences under low contrast, low luminance, off-axis, peripheral-zone, or short-term testing.

Purpose: support the need to look beyond central VA.

### Bucket 3: Dynamic / Real-World Task Gap

Studies testing or failing to test reading, visual search, mobility, sport, stairs, classroom transitions, or dynamic visual tasks.

Purpose: identify the biggest evidence gap.

### Bucket 4: Comfort / Quality of Vision / Adherence

Studies or reviews discussing symptoms, quality of vision, quality of life, compliance, adherence, and actual wear time.

Purpose: connect visual performance to practical effectiveness.

### Bucket 5: Design and Individual Differences

Studies showing results vary by lens design, gaze path, product, fitting, time, or child-specific factors.

Purpose: prevent product-wide generalization.

## Overclaiming Guardrails

Allowed cautious statements:

- Central visual acuity is important but not sufficient to fully represent real-world visual experience.
- Some myopia-control spectacle designs show condition-specific visual performance differences under low contrast, low luminance, off-axis, or peripheral-zone testing.
- Current evidence is incomplete for dynamic real-world tasks and long-term adaptation in children.
- Compliance and comfort matter because treatment efficacy depends on actual wear.

Not allowed without stronger evidence:

- Myopia-control spectacle lenses cause daily activity impairment.
- A specific design is unsafe based only on laboratory visual performance differences.
- Preserved central VA proves there is no functional visual cost.
- Adult immediate crossover findings prove pediatric long-term adaptation outcomes.
- A field observation score proves clinical severity, treatment effect, or risk level.

## Working Conclusion

The current evidence plan supports treating `DQ-KDF-001-002` as a legitimate discovery question. The most defensible next step is not to claim that children have real-world functional impairment, but to investigate whether central VA alone under-represents condition-specific visual performance, comfort, adaptation, and adherence in children wearing myopia-control spectacle lenses.
