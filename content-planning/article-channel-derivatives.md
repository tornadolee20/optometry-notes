# article-channel-derivatives.md

Updated: 2026-04-15

## Purpose

This workflow defines how a finished article should be translated into lighter channel outputs without mixing those derivative rules into the core writing or packaging skills.

The article remains the source of truth.

Channel derivatives are downstream outputs.

## Preconditions

Only run this workflow when the source article already exists in a stable draft.

Minimum condition:

- the main article argument is clear
- the core story or teaching arc is stable
- the main takeaway is not likely to change dramatically

## Source Of Truth

The source article should provide:

- central claim
- target reader
- emotional frame
- key examples
- one clear next step

Do not invent a different thesis for derivative channels.

## Derivative Priority

Create derivatives in this order:

1. article summary block
2. Facebook version
3. Threads version
4. short CTA variants if needed

## Facebook Derivative

### Goal

Turn the article into a readable, trust-building post with a clear emotional arc.

### Characteristics

- stronger narrative continuity
- slightly fuller context
- one main message
- one clean CTA

### Avoid

- sounding like a pasted article excerpt
- stuffing too many subpoints into one post
- generic inspiration language

## Threads Derivative

### Goal

Turn the article into a compact thread or single-post sequence built around curiosity, tension, or a strong insight turn.

### Characteristics

- faster hook
- shorter sentences
- sharper idea framing
- stronger line-by-line momentum

### Avoid

- long preambles
- over-explaining before the hook lands
- lifting article paragraphs with minimal compression

## CTA Derivatives

Only derive CTA variants after the article angle is stable.

CTA should match the article mode:

- educational article -> invite reading or saving
- service article -> invite consultation or discussion
- belief-shift article -> invite reflection or conversation

Do not force a sales CTA where the article mainly builds trust.

## Skill Boundary

This workflow does not replace the main article-writing skill.

Use it after:

- `uncle-glasses-writing-voice` for voice
- `uncle-glasses-writing-qa` for draft correction
- `uncle-glasses-blog-packager` for blog-ready article packaging

Use this workflow only for downstream derivative output.

## Output Rule

Every derivative should preserve:

- the same main claim
- the same audience
- the same trust level

Every derivative may change:

- length
- hook style
- pacing
- CTA intensity

## Bottom Line

The article is the mother asset.

Facebook and Threads are not separate ideas.
They are compressed, channel-native projections of the same core thinking.
