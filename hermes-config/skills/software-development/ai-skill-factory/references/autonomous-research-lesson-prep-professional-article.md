# Autonomous Research Lesson-Prep: Professional Article + Second-Brain Output

Use this reference when a research/lesson-prep workflow is being productized for 目鏡大叔 or the AI Skill Factory.

## Durable output requirement

After completing the research report, the workflow should also produce a rigorous Traditional Chinese professional article draft of about 2000 Chinese characters.

The article should include:

- A clear professional title.
- Introductory framing for the target reader.
- Structured body paragraphs grounded in the research findings.
- A conservative conclusion that separates evidence from recommendation.
- Source notes with DOI/PMID/source links when available.
- Explicit overclaiming guardrails: avoid claims such as “guaranteed,” “always works,” “no side effects,” or direct treatment selection without professional judgment.

## Second-brain archival pattern

Do not leave the research as a chat-only artifact. Archive it into Obsidian/second brain as reusable assets when the user asks for archival or when the research is intended to become part of the AI Skill Factory.

Recommended asset split:

```text
01-專家與MOC/
└── MOC-<topic>.md

04-知識卡片/
├── <date>-<topic>-證據地圖.md
├── <date>-<topic>-過度宣稱防火牆.md
├── <date>-<topic>-教學設計卡.md
├── 文獻卡-<key-source-1>.md
├── 文獻卡-<key-source-2>.md
└── 文獻卡-<key-source-3>.md

10-歷史文章智庫/
└── <date>-<topic>-專業文章草稿.md
```

For the MOC, link back to the original workflow run and the research template. For every card, include wikilinks to the MOC and the workflow run.

## Verification anchors

When verifying this class of output, check:

- Template requires a ~2000-character professional article.
- Workflow run links to the article draft.
- Article has frontmatter, title, source workflow link, source notes, DOI/PMID where applicable.
- Article contains overclaiming guardrail language.
- Obsidian archival outputs include MOC, evidence map, overclaiming firewall, teaching card, and key literature cards when archival is requested.
