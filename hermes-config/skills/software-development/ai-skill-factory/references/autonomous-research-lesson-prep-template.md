## Research Design Requirements

Before the research body, require the agent to define 3–5 research questions, including:

1. Current consensus.
2. Knowledge gaps for the target audience.
3. High-quality evidence available.
4. Debated or unverified claims.
5. How the topic can become teachable, applicable, and productizable.

Search strategy should include Chinese keywords, English keywords, synonyms/related terms, exclusion terms, search platforms/databases, time range, inclusion criteria, and exclusion criteria.

For clinical/health/optometry topics, choose a suitable framing table:

- **PICO** for intervention questions: Population, Intervention, Comparison, Outcome.
- **PECO** for exposure/risk-factor questions: Population, Exposure, Comparison, Outcome.
- **PICo** for qualitative/education/experience questions: Population, Interest, Context.

## Source and Literature Requirements

Prioritize:

1. Systematic review
2. Meta-analysis
3. Clinical guideline
4. Consensus statement
5. Randomized controlled trial (RCT)
6. Large cohort study
7. Professional association statement
8. Official documentation, white papers, or technical docs

Core literature table schema:

| Source / Literature | Author / Organization and Year | Study / Source Type | DOI / Link | One-sentence conclusion | Lesson-prep use | Evidence strength | Interpretation caution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Evidence Labels

- **High**: systematic review, meta-analysis, high-quality guideline, multiple consistent RCTs.
- **Medium**: single RCT, good cohort study, professional consensus, stable industry data.
- **Low**: case report, expert opinion, small study, early unreplicated finding.
- **Trend judgment**: insufficient direct evidence, but multiple signals suggest a direction.

## Overclaiming Firewall

Require a table that separates unsafe phrasing from safer public/teaching phrasing:

| Overclaim-prone statement | Why it is unsafe | More precise statement | Teaching-safe wording |
|---|---|---|---|

Check specifically for:

- Correlation being presented as causation.
- Preliminary evidence being presented as settled fact.
- Product effects being generalized to everyone.
- Ignored sample size, population differences, follow-up length, or conflicts of interest.
- AI/tools being framed as fully replacing professionals.
- Foreign evidence being applied to Taiwan without limitations.

## Teaching Translation Requirements

Separate:

- Facts supported by sources.
- Inferences drawn from sources.
- Recommendations for teaching/business/content strategy.
- Unverified or still-debated points.
- Overclaiming risks.

Translate the research into:

- Student-friendly explanation.
- Parent/consumer-friendly explanation.
- Slide-ready key points with source pointers.
- Discussion questions.
- Blog/social/short-video angles.
- Skill Factory packaging candidates: skill, workflow, template, prompt, example, test case, reference, case library.

## Obsidian Placement and Card Types

For the user's active optometry knowledge vault, reusable research/lesson-prep templates belong under `05-營運SOP與模板/` unless a more specific MOC or domain folder is requested.

When converting a research result into Obsidian assets, propose card-level storage rather than only a one-off report:

| Note type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| MOC topic index | Navigation, overview, related notes |
| Core knowledge card | One concept per note |
| Literature card | DOI/link, study design, conclusion, limitations |
| Teaching card | Classroom explanation, analogy, interaction question |
| Case card | Scenario, action, result, teaching point |
| Social material card | Post/video hooks and reusable angles |
| AI Skill Factory candidate module | skill / workflow / template / prompt candidate with I/O and dependencies |

## Verification Anchors

When this workflow is written into an Obsidian template, ad-hoc verification should check at least: frontmatter/title, research-question section, search-strategy section, PICO/PECO/PICo section, literature table with DOI/link, evidence labels, overclaiming firewall, Obsidian storage schema, quick version, 目鏡大叔 version, and follow-up prompts for slide sources and Skill Factory metadata.
