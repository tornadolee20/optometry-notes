## Rules

- `VERIFIED`: directly supported by reliable evidence.
- `CONTESTED`: credible disagreement or incomplete evidence exists.
- `SIMPLIFIED_FOR_STORY`: materially true but compressed for narration.
- `VISUAL_METAPHOR`: illustrative image, not literal factual depiction.
- `UNVERIFIED`: not safe to present as fact.

## Release Gate

- [ ] Every historical / scientific claim has a Claim ID.
- [ ] Contested claims are narrated with appropriate uncertainty.
- [ ] Visual metaphors cannot be mistaken for documentary evidence.
- [ ] No generated historical image is presented as archival footage unless clearly labeled.
