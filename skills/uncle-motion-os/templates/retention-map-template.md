## Retention Rules

A beat is weak if it has none of:

- new question
- partial answer
- payoff
- emotional shift
- visual-language shift
- contradiction
- escalation
- reveal

## Danger Zones

Mark any block longer than 8–12 seconds that only explains.

For each danger zone write:

- why it slows
- what to compress
- what question should remain open
- what visual or audio change carries the viewer forward
