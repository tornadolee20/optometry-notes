## Retention Danger Zone

### Original 54–64s historical correction

Risk:
First major reveal has just paid off; a 10-second explanatory history block can flatten momentum.

Action:
Compress to ~5–6 seconds and move immediately into false ending / match cut.

### Science block

Risk:
Terminology can create a second lecture valley.

Action:
Use experience-first structure:
dark room -> slow adaptation -> only then reveal Vitamin A role.

Keep molecular labels minimal and offload precise detail to visual overlays.
