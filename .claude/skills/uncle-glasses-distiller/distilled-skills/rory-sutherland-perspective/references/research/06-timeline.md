# 06 Timeline

## Working Timeline

### 1988
- Joined Ogilvy as a graduate trainee.
- Important because it roots him inside mainstream advertising before the behavioral science layer.

### Pre-2010s
- Worked as copywriter and creative director at Ogilvy for over 20 years.
- Important because his thinking emerges from practical persuasion and commercial creativity, not pure theory.

### 2009
- TED talk: "Life lessons from an ad man."
- Publicly crystallizes his theme that psychological value and non-linear solutions are commercially decisive.

### 2011
- TEDxAthens talk: "Perspective is everything."
- Pushes reframing as an intervention in lived experience.

### 2013 onward
- Nudgestock established as Ogilvy's behavioral science event.
- Signals institution-building around applied behavioral science.

### 2018
- Ogilvy UK bio publicly describes Rory as co-founder of a behavioral science practice within the agency.
- Highlights "unseen opportunities," contextual tweaks, and invented/discovered media framing.

### 2019
- `Alchemy` published in the UK and US.
- Publicly consolidates his worldview into a portable framework.
- Nudgestock 2019 positions him as host of a leading interdisciplinary behavioral event.

### 2021
- Ogilvy announcement for Nudgestock 2021 quotes Rory saying that "every business question is a behavioral question."
- This is one of the clearest expressions of his mature public thesis.

## Timeline Interpretation

The arc is:
1. classical advertising craft
2. discovery that perception and context are under-modeled
3. behavioral science institutionalized inside agency practice
4. public intellectual role built around reframing value and decision-making

## Why the Timeline Matters

This is not the path of:
- a purely academic behavioral scientist
- a typical copywriter
- a simple keynote speaker

It is the path of someone who:
- started with persuasion craft
- added psychology
- scaled it into an operating philosophy

That is why he is so useful for trusted retail and expert service businesses.
