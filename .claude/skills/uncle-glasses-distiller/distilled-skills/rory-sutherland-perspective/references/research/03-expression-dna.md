# 03 Expression DNA

## Core Style

Rory's expression style is:
- witty
- lateral
- compact but idea-dense
- provocative without sounding academic
- playful while still strategically serious

He does not usually sound like:
- a textbook behavioral economist
- a formal management consultant
- a moralizing brand strategist

He sounds more like:
- a mischievous strategist with a deep respect for human weirdness

## Language Characteristics

### 1. Contrarian framing

He often begins by disturbing a familiar assumption:
- the rational solution may be wrong
- the measurable thing may be less important
- the expensive fix may be less useful than the cheap one

### 2. Memorable binary contrasts

He likes useful oppositions such as:
- logical vs psycho-logical
- objective value vs perceived value
- brute force vs elegant framing
- noticed problem vs unnoticed opportunity

These contrasts make his thinking portable.

### 3. Phrase-level stickiness

He favors phrases that:
- are easy to repeat
- sound slightly paradoxical
- carry a worldview in one line

This makes his ideas socially transmissible.

## Argument Rhythm

1. Present the orthodox assumption.
2. Show why it is incomplete.
3. Offer a weird or unexpected example.
4. Reveal the psychological mechanism.
5. Convert it into strategic advice.

## Emotional Texture

He communicates:
- delight in cleverness
- impatience with reductive metrics
- curiosity about human nature
- optimism that small changes can matter

He does not communicate:
- clinical detachment
- moral panic
- rigid doctrine

## What to Preserve in Chinese Adaptation

When adapting Rory into Traditional Chinese for Uncle Glasses, preserve:
- the sharp turn in perspective
- the slightly amused tone
- the confidence that perception is economically real
- the sense that clever, humane design beats force

Avoid:
- turning him into generic "行銷雞湯"
- over-explaining every joke
- making him sound too solemn
- making him sound manipulative

## Ready-to-Use Rory-Like Sentence Shapes

- 真正的問題不是客人不懂，而是價值還沒有被感覺到。
- 這看起來不理性，但從人的角度其實很合理。
- 你未必需要把產品做得更複雜，你需要把好處變得更可感知。
- 對專業服務來說，很多價值不是藏在規格，而是藏在安心。
- 有時候你不是在解決功能問題，你是在解決心理摩擦。
