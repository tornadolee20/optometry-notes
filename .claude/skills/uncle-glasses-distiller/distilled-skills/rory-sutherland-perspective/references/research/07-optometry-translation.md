# 07 Optometry Translation

## Why Rory Fits Uncle Glasses

Rory is a high-value addition because Uncle Glasses already has:
- Atul Gawande for humane care and decision dignity
- Feynman for clarity and translation
- Russell Brunson for value ladder and conversion structure

What Rory adds is:
- why people still hesitate after they understand
- how invisible value becomes visible
- how trust can be designed through context
- how wording, ritual, and expectation shift adoption

## Key Optometry Use Cases

### 1. Progressive and multifocal lenses

Typical problem:
- customers compare only price or vague "high-end" language
- real benefit is hard to imagine before use

Rory translation:
- sell reduced switching fatigue
- sell smoother daily flow
- sell fewer moments of visual irritation
- reduce fear of adaptation before discussing premium

### 2. Myopia control

Typical problem:
- parents understand the logic but hesitate on commitment, money, or uncertainty

Rory translation:
- frame it as future risk reduction, not just current correction
- frame in terms of avoiding regret and preserving future options
- give the parent a psychologically graspable story, not just a data summary

### 3. Premium coatings and specialty options

Typical problem:
- features sound technical and interchangeable

Rory translation:
- map each option to a felt daily nuisance it removes
- make the before/after contrast psychologically vivid
- avoid jargon-led explanation

### 4. Adaptation period

Typical problem:
- customers interpret discomfort as failure

Rory translation:
- pre-frame adaptation as evidence of recalibration, not evidence of a bad choice
- explain what the first week means
- use specific expectation windows and normalizing language

### 5. Follow-up systems

Typical problem:
- aftercare is treated as admin rather than value

Rory translation:
- the follow-up message is part of the product
- reassurance after purchase reduces regret and increases confidence
- thoughtful timing and wording can prevent unnecessary panic

## High-Leverage Changes to Test

1. Add a one-minute "what to expect in week 1" explanation card.
2. Rewrite premium lens explanations around life friction rather than component hierarchy.
3. Add family-facing phrasing for child myopia control decisions.
4. Re-sequence price presentation so value is felt before numbers appear.
5. Build a post-pickup follow-up message that names normal adaptation experiences.
6. Audit waiting, trial, and handoff moments for trust signals.

## Example Reframes

### Instead of:
"這款鏡片設計比較高階。"

### Try:
"它厲害的不只是規格，而是你一天裡會少很多那種切來切去、眼睛一直重新對焦的煩躁感。"

### Instead of:
"這副需要適應。"

### Try:
"前幾天有點不一樣是正常的，因為你的大腦正在學一套更省力的看法。我比較在意的是一週後，你會不會開始覺得日常切換變順。"

### Instead of:
"近視控制比較貴。"

### Try:
"這不是單純多花錢買今天清楚，而是花錢把孩子未來更麻煩的風險往回拉。"

## Boundary Rules

- Rory translation must stay subordinate to clinical truth.
- Never manufacture urgency dishonestly.
- Never use behavioral insight to hide tradeoffs.
- Always pair framing with real follow-through.

## Best Pairings

- `atul-gawande-perspective` for care, trust, and humane choices
- `feynman-perspective` for making complex optics understandable
- `uncle-glasses-writing-voice` for turning insight into natural local language
