# 國際權威雙眼視機能檢查與分析 - 權威來源

## 官方與專業組織

1. American Optometric Association. `Adult Eye and Vision Examination Guideline Peer/Public Review Document`
   - https://www.aoa.org/Documents/EBO/Adult%20Eye%20and%20Vision%20Examination%20Guideline%20Peer-Public%20Review%20Document.pdf
   - 價值：把 binocular vision、accommodation、heterophoria、NPC、stereopsis、suppression、vergence facility 納入完整成人檢查架構，並特別指出 Modified Thorington 的 repeatability 優勢。

2. American Optometric Association. `Accommodative Dysfunction`
   - https://www.aoa.org/healthy-eyes/eye-and-vision-conditions/accommodative-dysfunction
   - 價值：作為 AOA 公開衛教與臨床入口，連結調節與雙眼視功能失衡。

3. College of Optometrists Clinical Guidance
   - https://www.college-optometrists.org/clinical-guidance
   - 價值：支持 routine examination + symptom-guided functional assessment 的架構。

4. American Association for Pediatric Ophthalmology and Strabismus
   - https://aapos.org
   - 價值：strabismus、diplopia、兒童對位與感覺功能分流的重要官方入口。

5. Royal College of Ophthalmologists
   - https://curriculum.rcophth.ac.uk/attachment_tag/squint/
   - 價值：strabismus care pathway 與 childhood strabismus guidance 的官方入口。

## 核心研究

1. Scheiman M, et al. `Long-term effectiveness of treatments for symptomatic convergence insufficiency in children`
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18852411/
   - 價值：CITT 核心證據之一，鞏固 CI 的臨床定義與 outcomes framework。

2. Maples WC, Hoenes R. `Near point of convergence norms measured in elementary school children`
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17435536/
   - 價值：NPC 常模的重要參考文獻之一。

## 蒸餾原則

- 以官方組織與 guideline 為骨架
- 以核心研究補強「哪些 pattern 值得優先相信」
- 不依賴單一學派式口訣作結論
- 優先輸出 clinical pattern，而非只堆疊常模

## Evidence Ledger

### Stable Core

- symptom-guided history is the entry point to binocular vision assessment
- alignment, fusion, accommodation, and sensory status must be interpreted together
- near-task data often carry the most clinical signal in symptomatic BV cases
- diagnosis should be based on patterns, not isolated numbers
- manifest deviation, incomitance, and sudden diplopia trigger escalation

### Evolving Edge

- the best cutoffs for NPC, vergence facility, and accommodative tests across age groups
- concussion-related binocular vision pathways
- the modern role of fixation disparity and associated phoria
- interpretation differences across stereopsis tests
