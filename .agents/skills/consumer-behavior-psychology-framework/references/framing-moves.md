# framing-moves.md

Updated: 2026-04-15

## Purpose

This reference provides practical reframing moves after the friction has been diagnosed.

Use it to improve clarity and trust, not to manipulate.

## Core Moves

### 1. From Feature To Lived Outcome

Translate:

- specification
- mechanism
- category label

into:

- what daily life feels like
- what becomes easier
- what frustration is reduced

### 2. From Price Defense To Decision Logic

Do not start by defending price.

Start by clarifying:

- what problem this option solves
- who it fits
- what tradeoff it avoids

### 3. From Option Dump To Guided Comparison

Do not present five choices as if five is helpful.

Instead:

- narrow to two or three
- explain the real difference
- state which one you would recommend and why

### 4. From Pressure To Permission

When fear is high, pressure reduces trust.

Use moves like:

- normalize hesitation
- make questions welcome
- make the path discussable rather than forced

### 5. From Technical Certainty To Human Relevance

Even correct explanation can fail if it sounds too technical.

Translate expertise into:

- understandable tradeoffs
- concrete scenes
- practical meaning

### 6. From Irreversible Choice To Managed Adaptation

When people fear commitment, show:

- what adaptation looks like
- what follow-up exists
- what can still be checked, adjusted, or discussed

## Warning

Do not use reframing to hide real tradeoffs.

Trust grows when the framing is clearer, not when the risk is disguised.

## Bottom Line

The best frame is not the most persuasive-sounding one.

It is the one that makes a good decision feel understandable and safe enough to make.
